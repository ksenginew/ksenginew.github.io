# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 06, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You interact with Our WhatsApp-based Service. We use Your Personal Data to provide and improve the Service. By using the Service, You agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Definitions
* **Application (or "Tasks"):** Refers to the Google AppScript and WhatsApp API integration managed by the Company.
* **Company:** Refers to **Tasks**, located in Sri Lanka.
* **Service:** Refers to the communication and task management features provided via WhatsApp.
* **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified individual, specifically **phone numbers** and **interaction timestamps**.

## 2. Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

### Types of Data Collected
* **Phone Number:** We collect your WhatsApp phone number to send and receive messages via the WhatsApp Business API.
* **Interaction Data:** We automatically log the **last time you interacted** with our service to maintain session continuity and improve response relevance.
* **Message Content:** We process the text of messages you send to "Tasks" to execute requested functions.

### How Your Data is Stored
Your data is stored securely within **Google Sheets**, managed via **Google AppScript**. We do not use external third-party databases outside of the Google Workspace and Meta ecosystems.

### Use of Your Personal Data
The Company uses your data to:
* **Provide the Service:** To deliver messages, updates, and automated responses via WhatsApp.
* **Maintain Activity Logs:** Using the "last interacted" timestamp to manage session timeouts and service efficiency.
* **Contact You:** To send administrative notifications or requested information related to your tasks.

## 3. Data Sharing and Service Providers
To operate this service, we share your data with the following essential Service Providers:
* **Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp):** To facilitate the transmission of messages. All messages are subject to WhatsApp's End-to-End Encryption.
* **Google Cloud / Google Workspace:** Your data is processed via Google AppScript and stored in Google Sheets.

We **do not** sell your phone number or interaction history to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Opt-Out and Deletion
You have the right to control your data:
* **To Opt-Out:** You may stop receiving messages at any time by replying with the word **"STOP"** to our WhatsApp number.
* **Request Deletion:** You may request the permanent deletion of your phone number and interaction history from our Google Sheet by contacting us at **ksanthusa@gmail.com**.

## 5. Retention of Your Personal Data
The Company will retain your phone number and "last interacted" timestamp only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Service. We apply a maximum retention period of **24 months** of inactivity before data is automatically purged from our Google Sheets.

## 6. Security of Your Personal Data
The security of Your Personal Data is important to Us. We utilize the built-in security features of Google Workspace and the Meta Business platform to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 16.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, You can contact us:
